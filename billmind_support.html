Support – BillMind

Help, contact and information about the BillMind application

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This page provides support information for the BillMind application. If you have a question, encounter a problem or want to send feedback, you can contact the developer using the email address below.

1. About the app

BillMind is a personal budget planning application designed to help users organize their finances in a simple and clear way.

The app allows you to manage income, expenses, upcoming payments, savings goals and monthly budget information in one place.

BillMind includes features such as:

  • tracking monthly income and expenses,
  • adding one-time and recurring income,
  • adding one-time and recurring expenses,
  • organizing expenses into categories,
  • tracking upcoming payments,
  • marking payments as paid,
  • payment reminders and local notifications,
  • creating multiple savings goals,
  • setting savings priorities,
  • planning monthly savings contributions,
  • tracking savings progress,
  • budget statistics and financial summaries,
  • expense category statistics,
  • savings history and progress tracking,
  • light and dark themes,
  • multiple language support,
  • support for multiple currencies.

4. Common issues

The app does not open or freezes

Try closing BillMind completely and opening it again. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure that you have the latest version of BillMind installed.

My financial data disappeared

BillMind stores its main financial data locally on your device. Removing the application, clearing its application data, resetting the device or certain system operations may remove locally stored information.

Important: Uninstalling BillMind may remove locally stored budget, income, expense and savings goal information.

I cannot add an income or expense

Make sure all required fields have been completed correctly. The entered amount must be a valid number and must not exceed the limits supported by the application.

A payment reminder did not appear

Make sure notifications are enabled for BillMind in your device settings. Also check that reminders are enabled for the selected expense and that the reminder time has been configured correctly.

Operating system settings such as notification permissions, Focus modes or battery restrictions may affect the delivery of notifications.

The notification is displayed in the wrong language

BillMind uses the currently selected application language when creating payment reminders.

After changing the application language, save the settings. BillMind will update active payment reminders using the newly selected language.

The language did not change immediately

After selecting a different language in BillMind settings, save the changes. If some interface elements are not refreshed immediately, close and reopen the application.

The selected theme is not displayed correctly

BillMind supports light, dark and system themes. If the system theme is selected, the appearance of the app follows your device settings.

The currency changed but the amounts did not

Changing the currency in BillMind changes how monetary values are displayed.

BillMind does not automatically convert existing amounts using foreign exchange rates.

My savings goal does not receive the full planned amount

BillMind calculates how much money is available after income, expenses and the configured safety amount are taken into account.

If there is not enough money available to fully fund all savings goals, the app may distribute available funds according to the priorities assigned to your goals.

5. Accounts and login

BillMind does not require creating an account or logging in to use its main personal budgeting features.

You can start using the app and creating your budget immediately after opening BillMind.

6. Bank accounts

BillMind does not require connecting a bank account to manage your budget.

Income, expenses, payments and savings goals are entered and managed by the user inside the application.

7. Data storage

BillMind is designed to store its main application data locally on your device.

This may include information such as:

  • income entries,
  • expenses,
  • payment status information,
  • savings goals,
  • savings contributions,
  • budget settings,
  • selected currency,
  • language and appearance preferences.
Important: Because financial information is stored locally, deleting the application may also remove data stored by BillMind.

8. Notifications

BillMind can use local notifications to remind you about upcoming payments.

You can configure reminders for individual expenses and select the preferred reminder time in the application settings.

Notification permission can be changed at any time in your device settings.

9. Languages

BillMind supports multiple languages. The application language can be changed from the settings screen.

On the first launch, BillMind may automatically select a supported language based on your device settings. If the detected language is not supported, English may be used.

10. Currency

BillMind supports multiple currencies. You can select the currency used for displaying monetary amounts in the application settings.

Changing the currency does not convert previously entered values. For example, changing the currency from EUR to USD changes the displayed currency designation but does not perform an exchange-rate conversion.

11. Privacy

BillMind does not require users to create an account or provide an email address in order to use the main personal budgeting features.

You can read the full BillMind privacy policy here:

Privacy Policy – BillMind

12. Data deletion

Financial entries can be removed or edited from within BillMind where the relevant functionality is available.

If you want to remove all locally stored BillMind data, uninstalling the application from your device will remove the application's local data according to the operating system's normal application removal process.

13. Financial information disclaimer

BillMind is a personal budgeting and organizational tool. It is not a bank, financial institution, investment platform or financial advisory service.

Calculations, statistics, projections and savings estimates displayed in the application are provided for informational and organizational purposes only.

14. Feedback and suggestions

Feedback is welcome. If you have an idea for a new feature, visual improvement, language correction or any other suggestion for BillMind, you can send a message by email.
