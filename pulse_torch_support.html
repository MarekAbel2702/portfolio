Support – PulseTorch

Help, contact and basic information about the application

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This page provides support information for the PulseTorch application. If you have a question, found a problem, or want to send feedback, you can contact the developer using the email address below.

1. About the app

PulseTorch is a simple wellness application that helps users estimate their pulse, recovery, body load, calm level and arousal level using the phone’s rear camera and flashlight.

To start a measurement, place your fingertip over the rear camera and flashlight, keep still during the measurement, and wait for the app to analyze brightness changes from your finger.

The application includes features such as:

  • approximate pulse / heart rate estimate,
  • recovery indicator,
  • body load indicator,
  • calm and arousal estimates,
  • rhythm and stability feedback,
  • signal quality indicator,
  • camera preview to help position your finger correctly,
  • simple measurement summary,
  • tips for improving measurement accuracy,
  • clean dark interface,
  • multiple language support,
  • local processing on the device.

4. Common issues

The app does not open or freezes

Try closing the application completely and opening it again. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure you have the latest version of the app installed.

The camera does not start

Make sure the app has permission to use the camera. You can check camera permission in your device settings. If permission is disabled, enable it and open PulseTorch again.

The measurement shows 0 BPM or low signal quality

Make sure your fingertip fully covers the rear camera and flashlight area. Hold your finger still and do not press too hard. Try measuring while sitting calmly and repeat the measurement after a short break.

The result changes between measurements

PulseTorch provides approximate wellness measurements. Results may vary depending on camera quality, lighting, finger movement, skin contact, phone model and measurement stability.

The camera preview is dark

The preview may become dark or red when your finger covers the camera. This is expected during measurement. If the preview stays black before covering the camera, check camera permission or restart the app.

5. Accounts and login

PulseTorch does not require creating an account or logging in. The app is designed to work locally on the user’s device.

6. Privacy

PulseTorch does not require users to provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or health profile information.

Camera data is processed locally on the device during measurement. The app does not save photos, videos or camera frames and does not upload camera data to any server.
