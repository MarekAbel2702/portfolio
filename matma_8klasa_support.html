Support – Matma dla ciebie

Help, contact and basic information about the application

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This page provides support information for the Matma dla ciebie application. If you have a question, found a problem, or want to send feedback, you can contact the developer using the email address below.

1. About the app

Matma dla ciebie is an educational mathematics application for students in grades 4–8. The app helps users learn math step by step, solve exercises, review topics, complete section tests, and prepare for the Polish eighth-grade mathematics exam.

The application includes features such as:

  • math lessons with short theory,
  • examples explained step by step,
  • multiple-choice exercises,
  • section tests,
  • a trial exam mode,
  • quick revision cards,
  • saved exercises for review,
  • local progress tracking,
  • grade and difficulty selection,
  • light, dark, and system theme modes.

3. What to include in your message

To help us understand and solve your issue faster, please include the following information when contacting support:

  • the name of the app: Matma dla ciebie,
  • your device model,
  • your operating system version,
  • a short description of the problem,
  • steps that caused the issue, if possible,
  • a screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

4. Common issues

The app does not open or freezes

Try closing the application completely and opening it again. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure you have the latest version of the app installed.

Progress or settings are missing

The app stores progress and settings locally on the device. If the app was deleted, reinstalled, or app data was cleared, saved progress may have been removed.

Exercises or lessons do not appear

Make sure the selected grade and difficulty level are not filtering out the content you want to see. You can change these options in the app settings.

5. Accounts and login

Matma dla ciebie does not require creating an account or logging in. The app is designed to work locally on the user’s device.

6. Privacy

The application does not require users to provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or school name.

Learning progress and settings are stored locally on the device and are not automatically sent to the developer.
