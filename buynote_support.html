Support – BuyNote

Help, contact and basic information about the application

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This page provides support information for the BuyNote application. If you have a question, found a problem, or want to send feedback, you can contact the developer using the email address below.

1. About the app

BuyNote is a simple and convenient shopping list application designed to make everyday shopping easier.

Users can quickly add products to their shopping list, specify the required quantity and mark products as purchased while shopping.

The application includes features such as:

  • quickly adding products to a shopping list,
  • setting the quantity of each product,
  • marking products as purchased,
  • automatically crossing out purchased products,
  • showing the number of products to buy and already purchased,
  • shopping progress tracking,
  • deleting individual products,
  • removing all purchased products,
  • clearing the entire shopping list,
  • automatic local saving of the shopping list,
  • light and dark themes,
  • multiple language support.

4. Common issues

The app does not open or freezes

Try closing BuyNote completely and opening it again. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure you have the latest version of the application installed.

My shopping list disappeared

BuyNote stores the shopping list locally on your device. Removing the application, clearing its application data, resetting the device, or certain system operations may remove locally stored information.

I cannot add a product

Make sure that a product name has been entered correctly. If you also specify a quantity, make sure that the value entered is valid.

The quantity is not accepted

The quantity field accepts numeric values. If the entered value is not accepted, remove any unsupported characters and enter the quantity again.

I cannot remove an item

Individual products can be removed from the shopping list. Depending on the current version of the app, you may need to swipe the item to reveal the delete option.

The language did not change immediately

After changing the application language in BuyNote settings, save the changes. If some text is not updated immediately, close and reopen the application.

The selected theme is not displayed correctly

BuyNote supports light, dark and system themes. If the system theme is selected, the appearance of the app follows your device settings.

5. Accounts and login

BuyNote does not require creating an account or logging in. You can start using the shopping list immediately after opening the application.

6. Data storage

BuyNote is designed to store your shopping list locally on your device.

The main shopping list features do not require an online account or cloud synchronization.

Important: Because the shopping list is stored locally, deleting the application may also remove the data stored by BuyNote.

7. Privacy

BuyNote does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as an email address in order to use the main shopping list features.

You can read the full privacy policy here:

Privacy Policy – BuyNote

8. Data deletion

Products can be removed individually from the shopping list. BuyNote also provides options for removing purchased products or clearing the entire list.

If you want to remove all locally stored application data, you can clear the shopping list and, if necessary, uninstall BuyNote from your device.

Feedback is welcome. If you have an idea for a new feature, visual improvement, language correction or other suggestion for BuyNote, you can send a message by email.
