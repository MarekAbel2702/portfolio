Privacy Policy – Matma dla ciebie

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when using the Matma dla ciebie application and how user privacy is protected.

Matma dla ciebie is an educational mathematics application designed to help users learn math step by step, solve exercises, review topics, complete section tests, and prepare for the Polish eighth-grade mathematics exam.

1. What data do we collect?

The Matma dla ciebie application does not collect any personal data from users. We do not gather, store, sell, or process any information that could directly identify the user.

The application does not require creating an account, logging in, or providing personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, school name, or address.

2. Learning data and progress

Matma dla ciebie may store learning-related data locally on the user’s device in order to provide core educational functionality.

The application may store data such as:

  • selected school grade
  • selected difficulty level
  • selected app theme, for example light, dark, or system mode
  • section test results
  • practice results
  • trial exam results
  • best scores
  • last scores
  • average scores
  • dates of completed tests or exams
  • topics or sections recommended for review
  • exercise IDs saved for revision
  • local app preferences

This data is processed locally on the user’s device. It is not uploaded to any server, transmitted over the internet, or shared with third parties by the application.

3. Saved revision exercises

Matma dla ciebie allows users to mark selected math exercises as exercises to review later. The application may store the identifiers of those exercises locally on the device.

The saved revision list is used only to show the user which exercises should be repeated. The application does not send this list to us, to external servers, or to third-party services.

Users may remove exercises from the revision list inside the application. If the user deletes the application or clears app data from the device settings, locally saved revision data may be removed.

4. Educational content

Matma dla ciebie includes educational math content such as lessons, examples, explanations, exercises, section tests, and trial exam questions.

Reading lessons, solving exercises, and using educational content does not require an account and does not send learning activity to us.

The application does not track which lessons the user reads or which exercises the user solves on any external server.

5. Local storage

The application may use local device storage to remember user preferences and progress. This helps the user return to the app later and continue learning with saved settings and results.

Local storage may include:

  • selected grade
  • selected difficulty
  • selected theme
  • test and exam progress
  • revision exercise list
  • basic app settings

This information remains on the user’s device and is not automatically shared with us.

6. Ads and analytics

The application:

  • does not display ads,
  • does not use analytics tools,
  • does not integrate third-party tracking services,
  • does not track users,
  • does not sell user data.

7. App permissions

Matma dla ciebie does not require access to sensitive device features in order to provide its main functionality.

The application does not require access to:

  • camera
  • microphone
  • location
  • contacts
  • messages
  • photos or media library
  • calendar
  • health data
  • or any other unrelated system data

If a future version of the application introduces new features requiring additional permissions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Internet connection

Matma dla ciebie is designed to work without collecting or transmitting personal data. The application does not upload test results, learning progress, selected grade, selected difficulty, or saved revision exercises to the internet.

If the application is distributed through an app store, the store provider may process certain technical, purchase-related, diagnostic, or account-related information according to its own privacy policy. Matma dla ciebie does not control such processing.

9. Sharing data with third parties

Matma dla ciebie does not share personal data with third parties, because the application does not collect personal data.

Learning progress, test results, exam results, selected settings, and saved revision exercises are not shared automatically. If the user manually screenshots, exports, sends, or shares content outside the application, that action is controlled by the user.

10. Security

Since Matma dla ciebie stores learning progress and settings locally on the device and does not transmit them to external servers, the risk of external data interception is minimized.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their own device, especially if other people also use the same device.

11. Children’s privacy

Matma dla ciebie is an educational mathematics application that may be used by students, including younger users. The application does not require account creation and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or minors.

The application does not ask children to provide names, email addresses, location, school name, photos, contacts, or any other personal information.

If the application is used by younger users, parents or guardians are encouraged to supervise general device usage and app installation settings.

12. Educational disclaimer

Matma dla ciebie provides mathematics lessons, exercises, examples, test questions, explanations, and exam preparation materials for general educational purposes only.

The application is intended to support learning, but it does not replace school lessons, a teacher, tutoring, official exam materials, or individual educational advice.

The trial exam and section tests available in the application are training tools and may not reflect the exact structure, difficulty, scoring rules, or content of any official examination.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any new version will be published with an updated date at the top of this page.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy from time to time to stay informed about how privacy is protected in Matma dla ciebie.
